How we ride

The ride code

Simple rules that bring everyone home. Safety, respect and togetherness come first.

  • Wear complete riding gear.Riding jacket, riding pants, full-face helmet and gloves are mandatory for every ride.
  • Support every club member.Encourage female riders, respect everyone and help create a welcoming riding community.
  • No overspeeding.Ride responsibly. Reckless riding and racing are not accepted.
  • Follow riding rules.Following traffic rules and the ride instructions is mandatory throughout every ride.
  • Follow the ride leaders.Road Captains, Marshals and the Sweep rider will guide the group. Follow their instructions and signals.
  • Hold your position.Stay in formation, maintain a safe distance and avoid sudden or unsafe movements.
  • Respect the road.Obey traffic signals, road signs and speed limits.
  • Ride sober.No alcohol or intoxicating substances before or during a ride.
  • Help each other.A rider in trouble is everyone’s priority. Stop safely and support fellow riders when needed.
  • Leave no one behind.Stay with the group and follow the Sweep rider’s guidance.

ROAD CAPTAINS • MARSHALS • SWEEP — RIDER SAFETY IS WHAT MATTERS MOST TO US.
